Photos for the small client circles beside "Trusted by 15+ clients" at the top of the home page.

Drop .jpg, .png, .webp, .avif or .gif files into this folder:

- They fill the circles in file-name order, left to right: 01, 02, 03, 04. Only the first four are used.
- Circles without a photo of yours keep their cartoon face.
- Square photos with the face in the middle work best; others are cropped to the circle from the center.
- Any size works: photos are resized automatically.
- Optional: words after the number name the person for screen readers, e.g. "01 Jane Cruz.jpg".

Reload the home page to see them. The live site picks them up after you commit.
