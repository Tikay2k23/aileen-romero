Images for the intro: the 20 small cards that fly together when the home page first opens.

Drop .jpg, .png, .webp, .avif or .gif files into this folder:

- They fill the cards in file-name order: 01, 02, 03 ... Up to 20 are used.
- Fewer than 20? They repeat to fill all the cards.
- The cards are tall (about 12:17), so portrait images fit best; others are cropped to fill.
- Any size works: images are resized automatically.
- Take all your images out to go back to the stock ones.

To see them, reload the home page (the intro plays when the page first loads).
The live site picks them up after you commit.
