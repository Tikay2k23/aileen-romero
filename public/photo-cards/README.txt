Photos for the five cards fanned out under the "Download My CV" button on the home page.

Drop .jpg, .png, .webp, .avif or .gif files into this folder:

- They go on the cards in file-name order, left to right: 01, 02, 03, 04, 05.
  The 3rd one sits in the middle, in front of the others: a good spot for your best photo.
- Only the first five are used. Cards without a photo of yours keep their placeholder photo.
- Portrait photos fit best, about 7:10 (e.g. 1400 x 2000). Others are cropped to fill the card.
- Any size works: photos are resized automatically.
- Optional: words after the number describe the photo for screen readers,
  e.g. "03 Me at a client workshop.jpg".

Reload the home page to see them. The live site picks them up after you commit.
