Photos and words for "What Clients Say" on the home page.

1. Open testimonials.txt (in this folder) and write a block for each client: their number, name,
   role and company, and quote. How is explained inside.
2. Put each client's photo here, its name starting with the same number: "01 Jane.jpg", "02 Mark.png".
   Square or portrait photos with the face in the middle work best. Any size works.
   A client without a photo shows their initials.

As soon as one client is written in, your testimonials replace the placeholders.
Reload the home page to see them. The live site picks them up after you commit.
