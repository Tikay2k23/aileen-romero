Images for the cards on the Engineering page (/work/engineering).

Drop .jpg, .png, .webp, .avif or .gif files into this folder:

- They go on the cards in file-name order: 01, 02, 03 ... up to 15 (three rows of five).
- A file's name becomes its card's title: "01 Lead Routing.png" shows as "Lead Routing".
  A name that is only a number ("01.jpg") keeps the card's current title.
- Cards without an image of yours keep their placeholder photo.
- Landscape images look best, about 5:4 (e.g. 1500 x 1200). Cards crop from the top-left corner.
- Any size works: images are resized automatically.

Clicking a card opens a preview with the whole image. To give a card a "Visit the app" button,
put its link in links.txt in this folder (how is explained inside).

Reload the page to see them. The live site picks them up on its next deploy.
